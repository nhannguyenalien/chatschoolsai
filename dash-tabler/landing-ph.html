FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What if the bot answers wrongly?

The bot answers only from information your business provided. When unsure it hands over to a real person. Every month the team reviews and fixes.

I am not good with technology. Can I still use it?

Yes. The team sets up everything. You send your price list and common customer questions, then watch conversations and bookings on the dashboard.

Will my data be mixed with other businesses?

No. Each business has its own workspace, separating configuration, chat channels, customers, and reward points.

Which languages does the bot speak?

The bot detects the customer's language and replies in the same language; many languages are supported.

Which channels can it connect to?

Your website and Facebook page. For other channels such as Viber or WhatsApp, staff will check support and get back to you.

How do I pay?

Choose a package in the Packages section and pay through Whop (in USD, the amount is shown on the payment page). The payment page has a few questions about your business; once you answer them, the team starts.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, effective at the end of the billing period. There is no long-term contract.
