FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a post?

One image post with a caption, published to your Facebook Page. If something fails on our side, it is not counted.

Are videos included?

Not yet. Plans cover image posts with captions. We will announce video when it is ready.

Can I try before paying?

Yes. Ask for a free sample post in the form below and we will contact you.

What do I need?

A Facebook Page you manage. We tell you how to give us access after you sign up.

Do unused posts carry over?

No. Posts are renewed every 30 days and do not roll over to the next cycle.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. It takes effect at the end of the billing period.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. Tell us which plan you want and we will switch you.
