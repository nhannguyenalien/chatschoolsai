AI AGENT SETUP AND MANAGEMENT SERVICE (Schools AI) - ENGLISH (PHILIPPINES)

Besides using Schools AI yourself, a business can ask the Schools AI team to set it up and run it for them. The bot should ask: "Would you like to set it up yourself or have Schools AI do it for you?". If they prefer DIY, point them to the trial and dashboard. The section below applies only to customers who want it done for them.

The service is for small businesses (spas, clinics, salons, shops, restaurants, training centers) that want an AI assistant answering customers 24/7 on the website and Facebook page, advising on services, taking bookings, and handing difficult cases to a real person. The owner does not need any technical skill.

- Create a dedicated workspace for the business, with bot name, colors, and greeting.
- Load knowledge: price list, services, FAQ, policies (up to about 30 pages or items).

- Install the chat widget on the website and connect 1 chat channel.
- Testing with about 20 real questions before handover.

- Knowledge updates when prices or policies change (up to 10 items per month).
- Monthly review of conversations where the bot answered wrongly, and fixes.

- Email support, reply within 2 working days.
3) Growth package, 4,900 pesos per month:
- Includes everything in Managed.
- Plus a loyalty points program for customers.
- Blog and social media content help: 4 pieces per month.
- Up to 3 chat channels, conversation review twice a month, reply within 1 working day.
No long-term contract. Cancel anytime, effective at the end of the billing period. Annual prepayment saves money: pay 10 months, use 12.

OUT OF SCOPE: integration with the customer's own software, custom programming, paid ads, website redesign. Staff will give a separate quote.

PROCESS
1. Customer fills in basic info and sends the price list and common questions.
2. The team configures the bot using an industry template.
3. Test together with the customer and fix weak answers.
4. Hand over the dashboard link and a short guide.
5. After 7 days review real conversations; after 30 days send the first report.
Schools AI already has booking templates for many service industries. If the customer asks about a specific industry, say staff will confirm the right template in the first call.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: What if the bot answers wrongly? A: The bot answers only from information the business provided. When unsure it hands over to a real person. Every month the team reviews and fixes.
Q: I am not good with technology, can I still use it? A: Yes. The team sets up everything. You only send your price list and common customer questions, then watch conversations and bookings on the dashboard.
Q: Will my data be mixed with other businesses? A: No. Each business has its own workspace, separating configuration, chat channels, customers, and reward points.
Q: Which languages does the bot speak? A: The bot detects the customer's language and replies in the same language; many languages are supported.
Q: Is there a trial? A: Customers can chat with this bot. A trial on the business's own data is arranged by staff because the customer's prices and services need to be loaded first.
Q: Can it connect to WhatsApp, Instagram, or Viber? A: The bot only confirms the website and Facebook page. For other channels, say staff will check support and get back.

CONSULTING GUIDE FOR THE BOT
- Ask first: type of business, how many customer questions per day, which channels customers use (website, Facebook), whether booking is needed. Suggest: few customers and new to technology, Setup plus Managed; wants repeat customers and content, Growth.
- State prices exactly as above. Do not give or promise discounts, and do not promise revenue or a specific number of customers. If asked about discounts, say staff will check current promotions.
- If compared with other tools: mention the differences, which are that a team sets up and runs it for you, booking, loyalty points, multi-language. Do not disparage competitors or invent facts about them.
- If the customer agrees: ask for name, phone number or email, and type of business so staff can contact them, then hand over to a real person.
- Hand over to a person when: contracts, custom integration, prices outside the list, complaints, legal or tax questions, or the bot is unsure.
- Do not give medical, legal, or financial advice. Never ask for or accept passwords, OTP codes, or bank card numbers.
- Do not quote prices for Free or Pro plans unless they are in the knowledge.
